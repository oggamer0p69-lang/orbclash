About Orbclash — Free Ball Battle Simulator

Orbclash is a free browser-based ball battle simulator. Two balls, two boxes: each ball bounces inside its own box and destroys the platforms stacked at the bottom. Every ball starts at 1 damage and grows through its own ability. The first ball to break every platform in its box wins.

There are 15 ball types. 10 mathematical: Factorial, Fibonacci, Powers of 2, Prime Numbers, Collatz, Powers of 3, Triangular Numbers, Lucas Sequence, Catalan Numbers and Ackermann; plus 5 special balls: Speed Ball, Titan Ball, Splitter Ball, Gambler Ball and Hammer Ball. Every ball starts at 1 damage and its formula is shown in the game.

Features

  • 15 ball types: 10 mathematical formulas plus Speed, Titan, Splitter, Gambler and Hammer balls
  • Custom HP per platform layer
  • Box and circle arenas, 1 to 8 layers
  • Chaos mode: meteors, speed boosts, shrinking arena
  • Single-elimination tournaments with local leaderboard
  • Record vertical battle videos (MP4 preferred, WebM fallback) with sound
  • Seed system for reproducible battles
  • Presets, export/import JSON, dark and light themes
  • Works offline, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking

How the damage works

Each ball has an explicit formula. Hit number n (counting from 0) deals base × seq(n), so the first hit is always 1 and the damage keeps growing with no cap. Special balls add an ability on top: more speed, a growing body, clones, crits or smashes. Open the browser console and run __orbclash.matrix() for a round-robin win-rate table.

Frequently asked questions

Is Orbclash free to play?

Yes. Completely free, runs in your browser, no sign-up, works offline once loaded.

How many ball types are there?

15: ten mathematical balls (Factorial, Fibonacci, Powers of 2, Prime Numbers, Collatz, Powers of 3, Triangular Numbers, Lucas Sequence, Catalan Numbers, Ackermann) and five special balls (Speed, Titan, Splitter, Gambler, Hammer).

Can I record battle videos?

Yes. Records fresh battles as vertical MP4 videos (WebM fallback) with sound, ready for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

How do I add custom HP to each platform?

Arena tab → Platform HP → Mode = Custom → type any HP for each layer.

Does Orbclash work on mobile?

Yes. Fully responsive on Android and iOS with touch controls and dark/light themes.

What is the Collatz Conjecture?

An unsolved math problem: start with any positive integer. If it's even, divide by 2; if odd, multiply by 3 and add 1. The conjecture says every sequence eventually reaches 1. In Orbclash, the Collatz Ball runs this orbit and hits for the highest peak it reaches.

What is the Ackermann Function?

A recursive mathematical function that grows faster than any primitive recursive function. In Orbclash, the Ackermann Ball climbs the ladder from linear to doubling to 2k and beyond.

Can I play Orbclash offline?

Yes. Once loaded, Orbclash works offline. Your settings, presets and leaderboard are saved in your browser.
